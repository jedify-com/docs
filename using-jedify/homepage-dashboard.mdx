---
title: "Homepage Dashboard"
---

Jedify's homepage contains a dashboard of pinned widgets. Each of the pinned widgets represents a previously executed question in Jedify.


These pinned analyses can be used for frequently asked questions of your own, or commonly asked questions across the organization.


![](/images/docs/image-17.png)


### Adding Element To Your Dashboard


For any question and response in Jedify, you can pin the response to your dashboard by clicking on 'Pin To Home' as shown below.


![](/images/docs/image-18.png)


You can then define the timeframe that will be used to refresh this analyses.


If you are a Data Expert Role, you can also choose to share the pinned analyses with your entire team, by checking 'Pin to all organization users'.


![](/images/docs/image-19.png)


Once you save, the selected item will appear on your homepage and refresh on a daily basis.
