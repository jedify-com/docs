---
title: "Updating a Metric Definition After a Business Change"
icon: "pen"
---

### Scenario


Your finance team recently changed how Gross Margin is calculated and now excludes certain costs that were previously included. To ensure your Jedify reflects this new definition, you’ll need to edit the corresponding metric in the semantic model. This ensures alignment with current business rules.


### Steps to solve


1. Go to "Gross Margin" metric
2. Click "Modify" → "Edit base query SQL"
3. Modify the SQL query based on your new business filters
4. Save your changes
5. Once all changes have been made, click "Submit changes". Jedify will notify you when the new metric is ready for review.
6. Review the new metric: approve or decline changes
7. Verify the new metric: Ask Jedify questions about gross margin to ensure it aligns with the new business definition
8. Ready to go live? Click "Verify" to publish
