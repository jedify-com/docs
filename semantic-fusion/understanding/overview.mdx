### Overview


The Jedify Semantic Fusion™ Model, (SFM), is your gateway to transforming complex databases into AI-ready insights. This innovative framework serves as the core intelligence behind Jedify's agentic flow, enabling users to query complex data structures using natural language. The model constructs a rich knowledge graph where business entities and their relationships are represented through connected nodes, with each node containing contextual definitions and SQL implementations.


### Purpose and Value


Our intelligent agent aggregates multiple disparate data sources and seamlessly integrates them into a unified source of truth—a comprehensive knowledge graph that precisely defines entities and their interrelationships. This architecture:


- Establishes clear, consistent entity definitions and calculations, eliminating conflicting definitions scattered across systems
- Processes natural language queries through an intuitive conversational interface
- Synthesizes information from diverse sources including:
  - Database query logs for understanding usage patterns
  - Organizational terminology, jargon, and personal preferences
  - Business intelligence tools and dashboards
  - External APIs and third-party data sources
  - Document repositories and knowledge bases


### Key Benefits


- **Business clarity:** Through its intelligent abstraction layer, SFM empowers both business users and AI systems to explore and analyze data naturally, using familiar business concepts rather than wrestling with complex database terminology.
- **Consistency:** Ensures all users work with standardized definitions of business metrics and dimensions

 Flexibility: You maintain full control over your model, easily customizing and evolving it as your business needs change.
- **Universal translation:** SFM turns database complexity into business clarity, paving the way for smarter, more intuitive analytics that grows with your organization.
- **Self-Service Analytics:** Empowers non-technical users to perform sophisticated data analysis

 Reduced Development Time: Eliminates the need for custom report development


### Who is this for?


- **Data Engineers:** Who need to create and maintain consistent data models
- **Business Analysts:** Who need to access data without SQL expertise
- **Data Operations:** Teams managing complex data ecosystems
- **Business Users:** Who need to query data using natural language
- **BI Developers:** Professionals building visualization and reporting solutions
