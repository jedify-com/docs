---
title: "Editing Business Glossary"
---

This page explains how to create, edit, review, and publish changes to the business glossary.


Navigate in app: Semantic Fusion Model → Semantic Statements → Business Glossary


1. Click Edit to add or edit any content to your glossary.
2. Save your changes.
3. Test your changes by asking relevant questions in 'Ask Jedify'.
4. Publish your changes to be used across your entire organization.


**Please note: as with any changes to the Semantic Fusion model, your changes are only visible to data experts until they are published. **
