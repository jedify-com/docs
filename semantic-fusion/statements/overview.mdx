## Overview


Teach Jedify your organization’s language so the agent understands your domain, speaks your terms, and returns answers that match how you measure the business. Move definitions out of scattered docs and into Jedify where they drive every query.

## Audience


Built for data experts who own definitions, standards, and guardrails.


## Benefits


- **Consistent answers:** One place to define official meanings for business terms.
- **Language aware:** Map team lingo and legacy terms to standard definitions.
- **Time that matches reality:** Align queries to fiscal calendars, rolling windows, and cohort anchors you actually use.
- **Trustworthy guidance:** Align on settings across the business with maximum control.


## Components


### Business Glossary


A governed, category-organized dictionary of your company’s terms, metrics, and rules that gives Jedify the context to understand your language and answer domain-specific questions with precision.


### Synonyms


Language mapping so natural questions land on the right entities. Example: “customers” equals “users” and “GMV” maps to “Revenue.”


### Time Analysis


Rules for fiscal calendars, week starts, rolling windows (7, 28, 90 days), and cohort anchors. Prevent apples to oranges trends.


### Answer Guidance


Organization-defined presentation and response formatting. Set date and number formats and custom display preferences so answers match your communication standards and user expectations.


All statements are reviewable and versioned to provide governance without slowing teams down.
