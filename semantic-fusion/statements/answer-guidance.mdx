This page explains how to create, edit, review, and publish changes to the Answer Guidance.


Navigate in app: Semantic Fusion Model → Semantic Statements → Answer Guidance


1. Adjust any of the settings based on your needs OR add a new custom rule.
2. Adding a new custom rule:


![](/images/docs/image-11.png)
   - Click 'Add custom rule'
   - Type in a 'Rule Name' and Instruction based on your need.
   - Click 'Generate Examples' to generate 3 examples of questions and corresponding behavior related to the new rule.
   - Review the examples and select the ones that match the expected behavior. If needed, generate new examples or adjust the instruction to make it more accurate or clear.
   - Click 'Create Rule'.
3. Test your changes by asking relevant questions in 'Ask Jedify'.
4. Publish your changes to be used across your entire organization.


**Please note: as with any changes to the Semantic Fusion model, your changes are only visible to data experts until they are published. **
