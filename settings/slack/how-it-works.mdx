## Agent Mode in Slack


When you ask a question in Slack, Jedify answers with the same [Agent mode](/agent-mode) that powers the main platform. It:


1. Understands your natural language question
2. Picks the data it needs from your connected data sources
3. Runs the queries and analyzes the results
4. Returns the answer directly in Slack


This ensures you get the same quality of analysis whether you're using the web platform or Slack.


## Live Progress


While Jedify works, the answer streams into the thread. Each step it takes, such as finding the data or running a query, shows as a task that updates from in progress to complete. The answer text appears as it is written, and the finished answer ends with a link to the conversation in Jedify and the feedback buttons.


## Clarifying Questions


When a question can be read more than one way, Jedify asks before it answers. It posts a card with the options to choose from, and a text box when you can answer in your own words. Pick an answer and click **Submit**, or reply in the thread. Jedify then continues the same analysis, and the card shows the answer and who gave it.


## Session Continuity


- **Thread conversations:** All follow-up questions within a Jedify thread keep the session's context. In channels and group DMs, Jedify answers replies in its threads without a mention. See [Communication Patterns](/settings/slack/communication-patterns).
- **Platform transition:** Use the provided link to continue your analysis in the Jedify platform with full visualization capabilities
- **Context preservation:** Your analysis session seamlessly transfers between Slack and the main platform
