### Direct Messages (DMs)


**No @mention required** - Just type your question directly
`How many active accounts do we have?`


**Public/Private Channels & Group DMs**


**Always use @Jedify** to get the app’s attention
`@Jedify What's our current pipeline value for Q1?`


**Thread Replies**
**Always use @Jedify** even in thread follow-ups (unless in a DM where it’s not needed on thread replies.)
Initial: `@Jedify Show me Q4 sales data`
Reply in thread: `@Jedify Can you break this down by region?`


**Important:** Questions within an existing Jedify thread are treated as follow-ups in the same analysis session, maintaining context from the initial query.
