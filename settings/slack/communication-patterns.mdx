### Direct Messages (DMs)


**No @mention required** - Just type your question directly, at the top level or in a thread.
`How many active accounts do we have?`


### Public/Private Channels & Group DMs


**Use @Jedify** to start a conversation
`@Jedify What's our current pipeline value for Q1?`


### Thread Replies


Once Jedify answers in a thread, it follows that thread: every reply is treated as a follow-up question, so you don't need to mention Jedify again.

- Initial: `@Jedify Show me Q4 sales data`
- Reply in thread: `Can you break this down by region?`

**Important:** Questions within a Jedify thread are follow-ups in the same analysis session, so Jedify keeps the context of the earlier questions and answers.


### Muting a Thread


To keep talking in a thread without Jedify answering every message, mute it:

- Reply with just `mute`, or a similar phrase such as `stop`, `unfollow` or `stop following this thread`. Jedify confirms, and from then on answers in that thread only when someone mentions it.
- From Jedify's second answer in a thread, each answer has a **Mute this thread** button.

In a muted thread, `@Jedify` gets you one answer and the thread stays muted. To have Jedify answer every reply again, send `@Jedify follow this thread again`.

Muting applies to channels and group DMs. In a DM, Jedify always answers.


### Asking About the Conversation


When **Thread Context** is on, Jedify reads the messages before your mention:

- **In a thread:** the earlier messages in that thread.
- **At the top of a channel or group DM:** the recent messages in the channel (by default, up to 10 messages from the last 30 minutes).

After a discussion, you can write just `@Jedify` or `@Jedify what do you think?`, and Jedify turns the conversation into a complete question before it answers. A Jedify admin turns this on in the Slack settings. See [Installation](/settings/slack/installation).
