# Getting Started


When you first interact with Jedify in Slack:


`@Jedify introduce yourself`


![](/images/docs/image-35.png)


Jedify will respond with its capabilities and confirm it’s connected to your data.


# What You Can Ask


Ask the same questions you would in the Jedify platform:


- Business metrics: Revenue, customer data, usage patterns
- Performance analysis: Trends, comparisons, forecasts
- Deep insights (research questions): Root cause analysis, correlations, anomalies


### Example Queries


- “What’s our current ARR?”
- “Show me customer growth over the last quarter”
- “Why did conversion rates change last month?”
- “Compare product adoption across different segments”
