### Data Access


- Same data sources as your main Jedify account
- Real-time sync with your connected databases
- Respects all existing data permissions


### Slack-Specific Constraints


- Text-based responses only (no embedded charts)
- Message length limited by Slack’s constraints
- Requires @mention except in direct messages


### Best Practices


- Be specific in your questions for best results
- Use threads to keep related queries together
- Provide context when asking follow-up questions
- Always @mention Jedify except in direct messages
