### Data Access


- Same data sources as your main Jedify account
- Real-time sync with your connected databases
- Respects all existing data permissions


### Slack-Specific Constraints


- Text-based responses only (no embedded charts)
- Message length limited by Slack’s constraints: a very long answer is cut short, and the link to Jedify has the full answer
- Requires @mention to start a conversation, except in direct messages
- An answer can't be stopped from Slack once it has started


### Best Practices


- Be specific in your questions for best results
- Use threads to keep related queries together
- Provide context when asking follow-up questions
- @mention Jedify to start a conversation in a channel or group DM; in its threads, just reply
- Mute a thread when your team wants to discuss without Jedify answering
