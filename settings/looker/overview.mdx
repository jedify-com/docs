## Bring Jedify Inside Your Looker Dashboards

Jedify integrates directly into Google Looker as a native extension. Ask natural language questions about your data without leaving your dashboard — Jedify automatically understands your dashboard context, including active filters, tile queries, and live data.

### Two Ways to Use It

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Dashboard Tile" icon="table-cells">
    Embed Jedify as a chat tile inside any Looker dashboard. Ask questions in the context of the dashboard you're viewing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Standalone App" icon="expand">
    Access Jedify as a full-page application from Looker's Applications menu for a more spacious chat experience.
  </Card>

### What Makes It Powerful

- **Dashboard-Aware**: Every question is automatically enriched with your dashboard's filters, tile queries, SQL, and live data
- **Deep Research**: Uses Jedify's Research Agent for multi-step analysis — the same engine behind the main platform
- **Persistent Sessions**: Your conversation history is preserved across page reloads, scoped per dashboard
- **Shareable Results**: Every answer includes a link to view the full research session in Jedify
