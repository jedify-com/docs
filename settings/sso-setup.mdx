## Overview


Jedify supports enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO) authentication, allowing your team to securely access Jedify using your organization's existing identity provider. SSO enables centralized user management, enhanced security, and a seamless login experience.


## Prerequisites


Before configuring SSO, ensure you have:


- Admin access to your Jedify account
- Admin access to your identity provider (IdP)
- Your organization's domain verified in Jedify


Please follow the instructions of your specific SSO provider in the next few pages.
